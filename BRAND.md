# Quantum at Lehigh

A quiet, curious identity built around the existing QAL mark, Lehigh brown, and pale blue.

## Color

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brown | `#653819` | Logo, text, diagram lines, controls |
| Blue | `#c5dbf2` | Page background |

Let blue occupy most of the page; use brown to give it structure. Diagrams use brown wireframe lines, with a few lighter curves to convey depth. Avoid solid globe fills, shading, and halos. Derive secondary text and fine rules by mixing the two primary colors instead of introducing unrelated grays. The full-strength primary pair has a contrast ratio of 6.93:1. Secondary text uses 85% brown and 15% blue, approximately 5:1 against blue.

The reusable color tokens live in `app/globals.css`. The original logo is `public/qatlsvg.svg`; render its native brown without filters.

## Type and form

- Use IBM Plex Mono throughout, in regular and medium weights, including equations and captions.
- Pair the QAL logo with “Quantum @ Lehigh” in small type, beside the mark on desktop and beneath it on mobile.
- Make measurement results read like concise terminal output, with an observation number and the sampled state.
- Use the available desktop width: a large diagram beside a compact math-and-controls column. Stack these on smaller screens.
- Give the sphere depth with restrained meridian and equator curves. Use a small round state marker, 30° ticks, an angle arc, and basis-state labels for useful detail.
- Controls have square corners, thin outlines, and minimal padding, with larger touch targets on touch devices.
- Social links pair a plain text label with a small diagonal SVG arrow drawn in thin strokes.
- State changes are immediate. Avoid halos, animated decoration, and presentation effects.
